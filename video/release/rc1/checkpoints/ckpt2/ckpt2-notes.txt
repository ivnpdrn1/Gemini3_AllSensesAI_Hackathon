## Module Features
- `init()` - Safe initialization (no permission request)
- `requestPermission()` - Explicit camera permission request
- `captureEmergencyFrames(incidentId)` - Capture 3-5 frames or ≤3 seconds
- `captureClip(maxMs)` - Alternative: capture single video clip
- `stop()` - Stop any active capture
